DMCA Guidelines

alt.fun respects intellectual property rights and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA"). alt.fun does not necessarily store or archive all creator-submitted content—when we identify infringing content, we respond by immediately removing or disabling access to the content identified as infringing (including token metadata, images, names, tickers, and any associated comments or social content).

alt.fun does not have the authority or the ability to adjudicate allegations of copyright infringement. The DMCA requires alt.fun to act as a middleman, processing claims of infringement by rightsholders and counter-notifications from creators. Any dispute is solely between the rightsholder and the relevant creator(s).

Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe content on alt.fun infringes your copyright, please let us know by contacting alt.fun support and submitting a written notice ("DMCA Notice") containing the following:

  • Your contact information: name, physical address, telephone number, and email address.
  • Identification of copyrighted work: a clear description or link to the copyrighted content you claim has been infringed.
  • Identification of infringing content: information reasonably sufficient for our moderators to locate and identify the content (e.g., creator wallet address, token name, token contract address, URL).
  • Statement of good faith belief and accuracy: the following statement with completed information:

I, ____________, wish to state that:

I have a good faith belief that the use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;

This notification is accurate; and

Under penalty of perjury, I am the owner, or an agent authorized to act on behalf of the owner, of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

Where possible and appropriate, alt.fun will notify the impacted creator of the claimed infringement.

Response to Valid Notices

When alt.fun receives a valid DMCA Notice, alt.fun will:

  • Promptly review the content;
  • Disable access to the content insofar as alt.fun is able to do so, including removing or hiding affected tokens, metadata, and associated user-generated content from the alt.fun interface; and
  • Notify the content creator responsible for the content about the removal.

Note: alt.fun operates a non-custodial interface to onchain contracts. While we can remove infringing content from the alt.fun interface (including token listings, images, and metadata served by alt.fun), we cannot delete content from public blockchains. Tokens deployed onchain will remain onchain regardless of whether they appear on alt.fun.

If you (the creator) believe that a notification was sent that was a mistake or is based on misidentification, then you may submit a counter-notice to support@alt.fun containing the following:

  • Your contact information: name, physical address, telephone number, and email address.
  • Identification of content: information reasonably sufficient for our moderators to locate and identify the content (e.g., token name, contract address) including the date/time of removal.
  • Identity of the claimant that submitted the infringement notice.
  • Explanation for why you believe there was a mistake or misidentification.

Where possible and appropriate, alt.fun will notify the claimant of the counter-notice.

Repeat Infringers

alt.fun will terminate a creator's access to the platform if alt.fun determines that the creator is a repeat infringer of protected works. Receiving three separate "strikes" will constitute repeat infringement.

Policy Updates

alt.fun reserves the right to update or revise this policy at any time. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of any revisions.

Contact

If you are a rightsholder (or their legally authorized agent) and believe that content on alt.fun infringes your copyright, you may submit a DMCA notice pursuant to these guidelines to request that alt.fun remove or disable access to that content. Please email support@alt.fun.
