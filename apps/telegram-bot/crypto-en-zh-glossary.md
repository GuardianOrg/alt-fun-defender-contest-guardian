# Crypto English → Chinese Glossary (币圈中英对照)

Community-preferred terminology used by Chinese crypto natives, not literal translations.

**Type legend:**
- **Standard** — widely-adopted, used in official docs, exchange UIs, news, dictionaries
- **Slang** — community jargon, informal, often crypto-native or censorship-driven
- For rows with two forms separated by `/`, the type label lists them in the same order (e.g. `Slang / Standard` means form 1 is slang, form 2 is standard)
- **Every Slang form has a paired Standard form** — use Standard for whitepapers, docs, exchange UIs, news; use Slang for tweets, chat, social posts. "—" means no widely-used form exists in that register.

## Translation Style Notes

- **Keep English acronyms** for: NFT, DeFi, DAO, DEX, CEX, DYOR, FOMO, FUD, LFG, ATH, ATL, ICO, IDO, TVL, APY, LP, KOL, KYC, MEV, WAGMI, NGMI. Chinese speakers in the space use them directly.
- **Use slang over literal** when the slang is the community default: 爆仓 over 强制平仓, 抄底 over 低位买入, 矿工费 over Gas费 (both fine), 韭菜 over 散户投资者.
- **Avoid mainland-only or Taiwan-only forms** unless audience is set: 简体 prefers 区块链/智能合约/算力; 繁體 may use 區塊鏈/智能合約/算力 (same characters). 数字货币 (mainland) vs 數位貨幣 (Taiwan).
- **Censorship-driven slang**: 大饼 (BTC), 二饼 (ETH), U (USDT) emerged because Chinese authorities crack down on direct crypto talk — use these for casual/social-media tone, formal names for documents.
- **Register matters**: For formal docs/whitepapers/exchange UI, prefer Standard column entries. For social posts, chat, Twitter/X content, mix in Slang to sound native. A doc that uses 散户 reads professional; a tweet that uses 韭菜 reads native.
